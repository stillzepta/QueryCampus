PES UNIVERSITY - OFFICIAL ACADEMIC REGULATIONS & POLICIES (2025-2026)
Document ID: PESU-ACAD-REG-2025
================================================================================

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
1.1 Minimum Attendance Criteria:
Every registered student is required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course (theory and practical sessions taken separately) during the semester to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Examination (ESE).

1.2 Medical and Co-Curricular Condonation:
Students having attendance between 65% and 74% due to verified medical emergencies or officially sanctioned institutional representations (inter-collegiate sports, cultural fests, academic hackathons, and conferences) may apply for attendance condonation. Medical certificates must be submitted to the Office of Academic Affairs within 3 working days of resumption of classes. Attendance below 65% will strictly result in an 'NE' (Not Eligible) grade, requiring the student to re-register for the course in a subsequent semester or summer term.

SECTION 2: COURSE REGISTRATION AND CREDIT SYSTEM
2.1 Credit Architecture:
The standard undergraduate B.Tech program comprises 160 credits distributed over eight semesters. A full-time student is permitted to register for a minimum of 16 credits and a maximum of 26 credits in any regular semester.

2.2 Course Add/Drop Period:
Students may add or drop elective courses within the first 10 instructional days of the semester with the approval of their Faculty Advisor and Department Chair. Dropped courses will not appear on the official grade transcript.

SECTION 3: ACADEMIC PROBATION & DISMISSAL
3.1 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) Thresholds:
A student must maintain a minimum CGPA of 5.0 on a 10.0 scale at the conclusion of each academic year. Students failing to meet this threshold will be placed on Academic Probation for one semester.

3.2 Remedial Support:
Students on probation must attend mandatory faculty counseling sessions and are restricted to registering for a maximum of 18 credits during the probationary term.
