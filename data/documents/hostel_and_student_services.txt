PES UNIVERSITY - HOSTEL REGULATIONS & RESIDENTIAL CODE OF CONDUCT (2025)

SECTION 1: CURFEW AND NIGHT PASS PROCEDURES
1.1 Campus Entry Timings:
All hostel resident students must return to the campus premises before 9:00 PM on weekdays (Sunday to Thursday) and before 9:30 PM on weekends (Friday and Saturday). Biometric entry scanners will lock after curfew.

1.2 Late Pass & Overnight Outstation Leave:
Residents seeking overnight leave or outstation travel must apply via the Hostel Parent Portal at least 24 hours prior to departure. Parental confirmation via registered SMS/email OTP is mandatory before the Chief Warden approves the gate pass.

SECTION 2: MESS FACILITIES AND DINING SCHEDULE
2.1 Dining Timings:
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:15 AM
- Lunch: 12:15 PM to 2:15 PM
- Evening Snacks & Tea: 4:45 PM to 6:00 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM
Vegetarian and non-vegetarian food counters are segregated. Special dietary accommodations are available for registered fasting or medical requirements.

SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT & STRICT PROHIBITIONS
3.1 Zero Tolerance Ragging Policy:
Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, or mental harassment) is strictly forbidden under University Grants Commission (UGC) guidelines and Indian law. Violators face immediate suspension, rustication from PES University, and immediate criminal prosecution. Anti-Ragging Helpline: 1800-180-5522.

3.2 Prohibited Items & Substances:
Possession, consumption, or distribution of alcoholic beverages, narcotics, tobacco, or e-cigarettes on campus or in hostel rooms results in immediate eviction and disciplinary committee hearing. Electrical heating appliances (induction cookers, immersion rods) are strictly prohibited in hostel rooms.
