SECTION 1: ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICES & STUDENT DESKS
1.1 Finance and Accounts Office:
The finance office is located in Block A, Room 102.
Operating Hours: Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM; Saturdays 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
Services: Tuition fee receipts, scholarship disbursements, examination fee clearance, fine waivers, and refund processing.
Contact: finance@pesu.pes.edu | Extension: 2102

1.2 Office of the Registrar:
Located in Block A, Room 201 (Second Floor).
Services: Official degree verification, bonafide certificates, transfer certificates, transcript requests, and enrollment records.

1.3 Dean of Student Affairs:
Located in Block B, Ground Floor, Room 015.
Services: Student club registrations, grievance redressal, disciplinary committees, and campus event permissions.

SECTION 2: CAMPUS LIBRARIES AND KNOWLEDGE CENTRES
2.1 Central Library:
Located in the Golden Jubilee Block, Floors 2 and 3.
Operating Hours: Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM; Saturdays and Sundays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Circulation Desk: Each undergraduate student can borrow up to 4 books for a loan period of 14 days, renewable once online.
Digital Repository: Over 50,000 IEEE, ACM, Springer, and ScienceDirect e-journals accessible through campus Wi-Fi via PESU credentials.

2.2 Reference and Silent Study Reading Hall:
Located in Golden Jubilee Block, 4th Floor. Open 24/7 during final examination weeks.

SECTION 3: HEALTH AND SAFETY
3.1 Health Centre & Medical Dispensary:
Located in Block C, Ground Floor (Adjacent to Sports Complex).
Doctor on duty: 24/7 round-the-clock emergency care, ambulance on campus, basic medication and preliminary triage. Emergency Hotline: +91-80-2672-9999.
